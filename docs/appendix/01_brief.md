**Audience:** newcomers to the Netherlands who just got a job in a Dutch city and need a place to
live within weeks, like I did when I moved from Iran to work in data. The default view of the app
is my own situation: a job in Utrecht.
## Update after profiling (2026-10-17)

I expected housing values to fall steadily with distance from the city. They do not: around
Utrecht the 10-20 km ring is the most expensive (median 551-578k against 468k for a typical home
in Utrecht), and values only drop clearly below the city beyond about 35 km, with a large spread
at every distance. The shortlist also showed that the cheaper parts of Utrecht itself and of the
bigger cities nearby (Amsterdam, Almere, Amersfoort) are where lower values and private rentals
meet.

So the story changed from "move further out" to "look neighbourhood by neighbourhood, and know
that cheaper areas often have fewer private rentals or smaller homes".
